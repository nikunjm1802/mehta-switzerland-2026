Day by Day

The Itinerary

June 30

Departure Day 🛫 Booked

PHX → DFW (AA1139, 7:30am) → ZRH (AA200, 2:35pm). The adventure begins from Phoenix Sky Harbor. Next stop — Switzerland!

July 1 – 4

Lucerne 🏙️ Booked

AMERON Luzern Hotel Flora. Chapel Bridge, Mount Pilatus Golden Round Trip (Jul 2), Brienz Rothorn Steam Train (Jul 3), Swiss Museum of Transport & Old Town exploring.

July 4 – 8

Grindelwald 🏔️ Booked

Bergwelt Grindelwald Alpine Design Resort. Jungfraujoch Top of Europe (Jul 6, booked 08:41 arrival!), First Cliff Walk & Glider, Männlichen Panorama Trail. Stop at Heidi's Photo Chalet in Interlaken on the way!

July 9 — THE BIG DAY!

🎂 Nikunj's 50th Birthday! Birthday

Schweizerhof Zermatt. Gornergrat Railway at 08:24 (Priority Boarding booked!). Matterhorn Glacier Paradise. Birthday lunch at Chez Vrony. Stellisee Lake. Birthday dinner at Golden India Zermatt. Room decorated with cake, flowers & chocolate box. 🎂🏔️

July 8 – 11

Zermatt 🗻 Booked

Schweizerhof Zermatt — just 100m from Gornergrat station! Car-free village, Matterhorn views, Sunnegga Paradise & golden evenings strolling the magical village.

July 11 – 13

Zurich ✈️ Booked

Hilton Zurich Airport Family Suite. Rhine Falls boat ride, Lindt Home of Chocolate, Lake Zurich cruise, Bahnhofstrasse shopping & the best Swiss chocolate to bring home!

July 13

Homeward Bound 🛬

ZRH → LHR (AA6860, 7:15am) → PHX (AA195, 11:00am). Arriving home in Phoenix at 1:55pm with hearts full and bags full of Swiss chocolate!
